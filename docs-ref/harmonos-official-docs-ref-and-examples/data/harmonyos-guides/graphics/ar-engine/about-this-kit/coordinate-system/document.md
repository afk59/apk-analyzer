# Coordinate System

#### AR Engine Gravity Aligned with World Coordinate System

-   The camera's center at startup serves as the origin of the coordinate system.
-   The direction of gravity corresponds to the Y-axis: upward is +Y, downward is -Y.
-   Horizontal forward and backward movement of the device corresponds to the X-axis: forward is +X, backward is -X.
-   Horizontal left and right movement of the device corresponds to the Z-axis: rightward is +Z, leftward is -Z.

#### AR Engine Gravity Aligned with Northward Coordinate System

-   The camera's center at startup serves as the origin of the coordinate system.
-   The direction of gravity corresponds to the Y-axis: upward is +Y, downward is -Y.
-   The compass north corresponds to the +X axis, and south to the -X axis.
-   The compass east corresponds to the +Z axis, and west to the -Z axis.
-   The gravity-aligned northward coordinate system is a fixed coordinate system and is unaffected by changes in the device's pose.

#### AGP World Coordinate System

-   The camera's center at startup serves as the origin of the coordinate system.
-   The vertical direction of the device corresponds to the Y-axis: upward is +Y, downward is -Y.
-   Horizontal forward and backward movement of the device corresponds to the Z-axis: forward is +Z, backward is -Z.
-   Horizontal left and right movement of the device corresponds to the X-axis: rightward is +X, leftward is -X.

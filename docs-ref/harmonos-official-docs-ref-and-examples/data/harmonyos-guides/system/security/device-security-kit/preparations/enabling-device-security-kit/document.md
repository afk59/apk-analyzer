# Enabling Device Security Kit

Before enabling Device Security Kit, make [necessary preparations](../../../../../getting-prepared/application-development-preparations/document.md).

![](https://contentcenter-vali-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/pvt_2/DeveloperAlliance_scene_100_1/fd/v3/UPk4j3mTSI6OZSA2ZwB-_g/note_3.0-en-us.png?HW-CC-KV=V1&amp;HW-CC-Date=20260410T062006Z&amp;HW-CC-Expire=86400&amp;HW-CC-Sign=A3758939547D52961104E9DEF9B62BE1E91320F68CD7EE0E7624187613F6DEC3)

Device Security Kit provides DeviceVerify, SafetyDetect, TrustedAppService, BusinessRiskIntelligentDetection, and digital shield functions. You can enable one or more of the functions based on your service needs.

1.  Sign in to [AppGallery Connect](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/en/service/josp/agc/index.html) and click **Development and services**.
    
    ![](https://contentcenter-vali-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/pvt_2/DeveloperAlliance_scene_100_1/47/v3/EWlHBorxRdq0P8PmPxUIMA/en-us_image_0000002515108435.png?HW-CC-KV=V1&amp;HW-CC-Date=20260410T062006Z&amp;HW-CC-Expire=86400&amp;HW-CC-Sign=F055CE11ABADC4F09ECAEE59A973FDF6AC5DB4EAAA9848D87C47DA7DEA9EECF3 "Click to enlarge")
    
2.  Click the project for which you want to enable Device Security Kit.
    
    ![](https://contentcenter-vali-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/pvt_2/DeveloperAlliance_scene_100_1/77/v3/YwOXCj5CT7qTrVNJi3uwnw/en-us_image_0000002514988437.png?HW-CC-KV=V1&amp;HW-CC-Date=20260410T062006Z&amp;HW-CC-Expire=86400&amp;HW-CC-Sign=9F6934329759E265C472B2676C13DB2C46F0C7E8E1D44D72472C4E2DE563DDF4 "Click to enlarge")
    
3.  Click the **Manage open capabilities** tab and enable the required functions.
    
    -   **DeviceVerify**: To enable this function, select **Device status detection** and click **Save**.
        
        ![](https://contentcenter-vali-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/pvt_2/DeveloperAlliance_scene_100_1/2f/v3/8z1RfWF2SPG1L2gLZCaSUw/en-us_image_0000002482788476.png?HW-CC-KV=V1&amp;HW-CC-Date=20260410T062006Z&amp;HW-CC-Expire=86400&amp;HW-CC-Sign=BDED19DDFC2C1F0A9D69DB11DFAA7C3F37699E937F6954438ECFB7FA83A9B146 "Click to enlarge")
        
    
    -   **SafetyDetect**: To enable this function, select **Safety Detect** and click **Save**.
        
        ![](https://contentcenter-vali-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/pvt_2/DeveloperAlliance_scene_100_1/d6/v3/saCgVufRSpmIEl1rKeP8kA/en-us_image_0000002482908440.png?HW-CC-KV=V1&amp;HW-CC-Date=20260410T062006Z&amp;HW-CC-Expire=86400&amp;HW-CC-Sign=31A8258CC6149C1F882C8B2B8FAF88037D8635580A9319432A8379903AC347F8 "Click to enlarge")
        
    
    -   **TrustedAppService**: To enable this function, select **Trusted Application Service** and click **Save**.
        
        ![](https://contentcenter-vali-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/pvt_2/DeveloperAlliance_scene_100_1/40/v3/ndOaWr1hRmmPR6ADRG5BBA/note_3.0-en-us.png?HW-CC-KV=V1&amp;HW-CC-Date=20260410T062006Z&amp;HW-CC-Expire=86400&amp;HW-CC-Sign=54461CCF1CD55D5177206EB547F6E8E93F7A8508429B2CBD93788FECAE9A1C38)
        
        To enable TrustedAppService, you need to be on the trustlist first. Send your developer ID, company name, app name, requested service, and the application scenario to agconnect@huawei.com. AppGallery Connect operations personnel will review related materials. After your request is approved, the operations personnel will add you to the trustlist that can use the restricted service. The review takes 1–3 working days.
        
        ![](https://contentcenter-vali-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/pvt_2/DeveloperAlliance_scene_100_1/02/v3/6OkOySceR2eEXFY-CipS9w/en-us_image_0000002482908442.png?HW-CC-KV=V1&amp;HW-CC-Date=20260410T062006Z&amp;HW-CC-Expire=86400&amp;HW-CC-Sign=875F48C88C019A196278DE3C21B1348756BE3C44BB44A991AB55D32EA14A07E9 "Click to enlarge")
        
    
    -   **BusinessRiskIntelligentDetection - Fraud detection**: To enable this function, click **Request** on the right of **Fraud detection** to access the service. After the request is approved, select the service and click **Save**.
        
        1\. Register as a developer on [HUAWEI Developers](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/en/) and complete [identity verification as an enterprise developer](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/en/doc/start/edrna-0000001062678489).
        
        2\. Click **Request** on the right of **Fraud detection** to access the service.
        
        ![](https://contentcenter-vali-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/pvt_2/DeveloperAlliance_scene_100_1/e4/v3/6vFCbt1JTPWbz-0UkLnjQQ/en-us_image_0000002561897901.png?HW-CC-KV=V1&amp;HW-CC-Date=20260410T062006Z&amp;HW-CC-Expire=86400&amp;HW-CC-Sign=2F12976B192996A48CE4DCC963D3DD032C7BFC62507AFFACE22726A720CEF106 "Click to enlarge")
        
        3\. Provide the required information by referring to the template in the **Request reason** text box, including the developer ID, company name, app name, usage scenario, and legal basis for using the service. (To use the service, you need to declare personal data and its usage in the privacy statement of your app. For details, please refer to [Personal Data Processing Information](../../personal-data-processing-information/document.md). Upload the proof of the legal basis as the attachment.) Then, click **Submit**.
        
        ![](https://contentcenter-vali-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/pvt_2/DeveloperAlliance_scene_100_1/92/v3/f-_5ZgJBTeulPvWXD3UIyw/en-us_image_0000002530836282.png?HW-CC-KV=V1&amp;HW-CC-Date=20260410T062006Z&amp;HW-CC-Expire=86400&amp;HW-CC-Sign=74CC78C40D3F3B619A275F9B13DF6C40C2E8A25333FD070DF394F620AB0A34E6 "Click to enlarge")
        
        ![](https://contentcenter-vali-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/pvt_2/DeveloperAlliance_scene_100_1/41/v3/hjxRijRlRMSkiTl80OTJFQ/note_3.0-en-us.png?HW-CC-KV=V1&amp;HW-CC-Date=20260410T062006Z&amp;HW-CC-Expire=86400&amp;HW-CC-Sign=5C89381D91BDAAA05E65E57461FD6C3E007C1BE1FADFDFD7E5DB3415576A54C9)
        
        After the request is submitted, AppGallery Connect operations personnel will review related materials, which takes 1–3 working days. After the request is approved, you can save your service configuration.
        
    -   **Digital shield service**: To enable this function, perform the following steps:
        
        1\. Register as a developer on [HUAWEI Developers](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/en/) and complete [identity verification as an enterprise developer](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/en/doc/start/edrna-0000001062678489).
        
        2\. Click **Request** on the right of **Digital Shield Service** to access the service. After the request is approved, select the service and click **Save**.
        
        ![](https://contentcenter-vali-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/pvt_2/DeveloperAlliance_scene_100_1/f1/v3/iyvduhxzRoCJNpYnNg6X_g/en-us_image_0000002515108439.png?HW-CC-KV=V1&amp;HW-CC-Date=20260410T062006Z&amp;HW-CC-Expire=86400&amp;HW-CC-Sign=BB12BEE351FC0899518226CE549890A6EF6EA86DE619DCAAB498A708D42DAA24 "Click to enlarge")
        
        ![](https://contentcenter-vali-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/pvt_2/DeveloperAlliance_scene_100_1/ae/v3/W7VAtmzUSlSrdpWQMDj0cw/note_3.0-en-us.png?HW-CC-KV=V1&amp;HW-CC-Date=20260410T062006Z&amp;HW-CC-Expire=86400&amp;HW-CC-Sign=36ED45E8119AF7163B2A1265B961C739677D0EAE1A6253B7640C38D373247306)
        
        Enter the reason for requesting the digital shield service and its application scenario in the request dialog box. AppGallery Connect operations personnel will review related materials, which takes 1–3 working days. After the request is approved, you can save your service configuration.
        
    
4.  Request a profile (.p7b) file. For details, please refer to [Requesting a Debug Profile](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/en/doc/app/agc-help-debug-profile-0000002248181278).
    
    ![](https://contentcenter-vali-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/pvt_2/DeveloperAlliance_scene_100_1/f2/v3/a73RN71nQMiNGI5qaP-MHQ/note_3.0-en-us.png?HW-CC-KV=V1&amp;HW-CC-Date=20260410T062006Z&amp;HW-CC-Expire=86400&amp;HW-CC-Sign=0C5504BB0F8D6A826792FFCADBEC3C228FD2C32856685017B433BC54FB1AA242)
    
    After the service is enabled, you need to request a new profile (.p7b) file.

# Connecting an App on Super Home Screen to the Distributed Camera

#### Use Cases

Super Home Screen is a feature that enables seamless connectivity between Huawei phones and head units. With this feature, users can mirror their phone apps to the vehicle's central display and use these apps with greater ease.

After a phone and head unit are connected through Super Home Screen, if your app mirrored to the central display needs to use the head unit's camera (for example, in a video call or meeting scenario), you can integrate the remote distributed camera capability. Then, the local device (phone) can obtain the captured image from the remote device (head unit) and finally display the image on the central display.

#### Preparing the Environment

Establish a Super Home Screen connection between the phone and head unit. On Super Home Screen, open the app to be connected to the distributed camera.

#### Development Procedure

If your app needs to use the head unit's camera in the Super Home Screen scenario to implement functions such as video calls and meetings, please refer to [Distributed Camera Development](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/en/doc/harmonyos-guides/camera-distributed) for more details.

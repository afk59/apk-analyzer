# Why does the Instant Shape feature fail to recognize a shape?

When a stroke ends, the stylus/finger must stay on the screen for a certain period of time (300 ms by default, which can be customized in the app) to trigger shape recognition. You can also enable **Lift to create** on the toolbar of the suite. After this feature is enabled, users can lift the stylus to trigger shape recognition without a pause. Currently, Instant Shape does not support hearts and clouds.

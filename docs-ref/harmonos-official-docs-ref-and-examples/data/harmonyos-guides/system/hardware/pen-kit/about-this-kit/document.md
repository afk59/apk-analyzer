# About This Kit

Pen Kit is a comprehensive suite of cutting-edge handwriting capabilities that revolutionize digital writing and sketching. It provides a wide array of enriching features, such as brush effects, handwriting editing, point prediction, Instant Shape, global color picker, and stylus interaction.

Pen Kit currently features five capabilities: handwriting suite, point prediction, Instant Shape, global color picker, and stylus interaction.

#### Handwriting suite

It can be directly integrated into apps. After that, the apps will have the following features:

-   Canvas
    
    Supports features such as handwriting, handwriting saving, canvas zooming, and Instant Shape.
    
-   Toolbar
    
    -   Brush: Offers seven brush effects, including ballpoint pen, pen, pencil, marker pen, highlighter, mosaic pen, and laser pointer with five pen widths and over 100 colors to choose from.
    -   Rubber: Erases strokes, erases pixels, erases only the highlighter, and clears strokes.
    -   Lasso: Enables operations including select, move, cut and paste, copy and paste, delete, and resize.
    -   Other features: Supports operations such as undo, redo, and block finger input.

#### Point Prediction

Touch points can be predicted and drawn in advance, based on the writing track, reducing the latency. This capability is enabled in the handwriting suite by default, and can be integrated into your app on a separate basis.

#### Instant Shape

This capability can be triggered by holding the stylus/finger on the screen for a certain period of time when a continuous drawing path ends and then identifies the drawing path as a regular shape. It is enabled in the handwriting suite by default, and can be integrated into your app on a separate basis. Pen Kit can identify the following shapes:

#### Stylus interaction

Provides the capability of listening to stylus double-tap and squeeze events.

#### Restrictions

This kit is not supported on the Emulator.

#### Supported Devices

This kit is applicable only to phones, tablets, and 2-in-1 devices.

For details about the models of phones, tablets, and 2-in-1 devices that support stylus hardware, please refer to the [list of Huawei phones that are compatible with the stylus](https://consumer.huawei.com/en/support/content/en-us15869694/) and the [list of HUAWEI tablet/laptop models that support HUAWEI styluses](https://consumer.huawei.com/en/support/content/en-us00737675/).

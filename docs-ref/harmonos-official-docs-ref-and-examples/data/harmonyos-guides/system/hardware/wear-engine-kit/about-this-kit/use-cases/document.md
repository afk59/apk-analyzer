# Use Cases

#### Distributed Collaboration Between Phones and Wearable Devices

-   Receive notifications for important events on user wrists. For example, any notifications for schedules, medications, or tasks set in your phone app, can be synced to the wearable app. This means that users can receive and view important notifications on their wearable devices without checking their phones.
    
    ![](https://contentcenter-vali-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/pvt_2/DeveloperAlliance_scene_100_1/37/v3/Tyq5mKNFSS-AzoLvbEJD9A/en-us_image_0000002190453285.png?HW-CC-KV=V1&amp;HW-CC-Date=20260410T062025Z&amp;HW-CC-Expire=86400&amp;HW-CC-Sign=DA41AC08E919E1C8453E821D364F1AAD4523D9AC8C655629C7179477AB1D24BB "Click to enlarge")
    
-   Introduce brand new interactive experiences to user wrists. For example, when users use a phone app to stream videos or listen to music, they can use their wearable devices to perform remote controls such as pausing, skipping to the next video/song, or stopping playback.
    
    ![](https://contentcenter-vali-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/pvt_2/DeveloperAlliance_scene_100_1/5e/v3/2Y4eghppQqaoDFLTVqRubQ/en-us_image_0000002155046636.png?HW-CC-KV=V1&amp;HW-CC-Date=20260410T062025Z&amp;HW-CC-Expire=86400&amp;HW-CC-Sign=F9B25E26B746D14DEE82CFAEDC1B858A39D904CAD682275C72F87EFADC52D5A4 "Click to enlarge")
    
-   Achieve real-time collaboration between phones and wearable devices. For example, a user can start navigation from your phone app and then receive real-time instructions from the wearable app for when to turn left, go straight, or turn right. So the user doesn't have to take out their phone or hold it in their hand to check the route as they navigate.
    
    ![](https://contentcenter-vali-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/pvt_2/DeveloperAlliance_scene_100_1/61/v3/7zOBaSl8S9edFyO8sqL3ZA/en-us_image_0000002190567613.png?HW-CC-KV=V1&amp;HW-CC-Date=20260410T062025Z&amp;HW-CC-Expire=86400&amp;HW-CC-Sign=945341B04B306499C853E4750F73A2CC460DE17D2D6AD74C6F407AC90B3EA152 "Click to enlarge")
    

#### Device Virtualization Between Phones and Wearable Devices

You can develop your phone app without having to develop a separate wearable app.

-   Obtain real-time wearable status. For example, your phone app can obtain the wearable status of connection, wearing, and battery levels in real time, providing more value-added services for users.
    
    ![](https://contentcenter-vali-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/pvt_2/DeveloperAlliance_scene_100_1/21/v3/t8QtjZrvT7WYEFhblXzGjw/en-us_image_0000002155206396.png?HW-CC-KV=V1&amp;HW-CC-Date=20260410T062025Z&amp;HW-CC-Expire=86400&amp;HW-CC-Sign=8603AE41D908CE5B6A3DE9CFEAF9F19543CB4908F30B481C414D4C705700772D "Click to enlarge")
    
-   Wearable device sensor: The phone app can read sensor data (such as ECG, PPG, and ACC data) from the wearable device.
    
    ![](https://contentcenter-vali-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/pvt_2/DeveloperAlliance_scene_100_1/9c/v3/DECQQkvlTwCrAwGLC_1vRA/note_3.0-en-us.png?HW-CC-KV=V1&amp;HW-CC-Date=20260410T062025Z&amp;HW-CC-Expire=86400&amp;HW-CC-Sign=09295F681D425902227ED369C0CC431A6BB9F2A22FC0845BEB2B533E89613691)
    
    The sensors can only be used by professional research institutions due to the limited release.

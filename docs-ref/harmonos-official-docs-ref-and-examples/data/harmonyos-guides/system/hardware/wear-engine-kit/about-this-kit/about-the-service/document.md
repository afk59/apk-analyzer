# About the Service

Wear Engine is designed for developers of apps and services running on phones and wearable devices.

By integrating Wear Engine, your app or service on a phone can send messages, notifications, and data to a Huawei wearable device, as well as obtaining the status of the wearable device and reading sensor data. This also works the other way around, allowing an app or service on a Huawei wearable device to send messages and data to a phone.

Wear Engine pools the capabilities of both the phone and the wearable device, which include the phone's apps and services and the wearable's device capabilities, creating benefits for consumers and developers alike. It enables consumers to use their devices in more diversified scenarios and enjoy more convenient services, with a smoother experience. It also expands the reach of your business, and takes your apps and services to the next level.

#### Open Capabilities

Wear Engine currently offers the following capabilities that are centered around Huawei wearable devices:

1.  **Obtaining basic wearable device information (basic device capability)**
    -   Open to: both individual developers and enterprise developers
    -   Phone app: retrieves the list of paired wearable devices, from which a user can select a target device, and supports querying and subscribing to wearable device status information, including the connection status, battery level, and charging status.
2.  **App-to-app communications (basic device capability)**
    
    -   Open to: both individual developers and enterprise developers
    
    -   Phone app: sends and receives messages and files, such as images and music.
    -   Lite wearable app: sends and receives messages and files, such as images and music.
    -   Wearable app: sends and receives messages and files, such as images and music.
3.  **Template-based notifications on wearable devices**
    -   Open to: both individual developers and enterprise developers
    -   Phone app: sends template-based notifications to a wearable device.
4.  **Obtaining the wearable user status**
    -   Open to: only enterprise developers
    -   Phone app: queries or subscribes to user-related wearable data, such as heart rate alerts and wearing status.
5.  **Motion sensor (wearable sensor capability)**
    -   Open to: only enterprise developers
    -   Phone app: obtains motion sensor data from a wearable device and controls data acquisition through enable and disable commands.
6.  **Human body sensor (wearable sensor capability)**
    -   Open to: only professional research institutions within the enterprise developer category
    -   Phone app: obtains human body sensor data from a wearable device and controls data acquisition through enable and disable commands.
7.  **Device identifier**
    -   Open to: only cooperative enterprises within the enterprise developer category
    -   Phone app: obtains the SN of a wearable device.

#### Service Advantages

Wear Engine allows apps to quickly and conveniently connect to Huawei wearable devices, opening up possibilities for you to explore and providing users with a broader range of application scenarios, a more user-friendly service experience, and seamless interaction with wearable devices.

-   **Convenient management**: Makes cross-device collaboration a full-fledged reality, through real-time monitoring of device connection and status.
-   **Real-time reminders**: Allows for timely reminders such as for important events sent directly to user wrists.
-   **New ways of interacting**: Introduces brand new interactive experiences to user wrists, such as using a wearable device to control music playback and navigate.

#### Restrictions

## Supported Devices

-   This kit is applicable to phones and tablets.
-   Support for wearables was introduced in version 5.1.0(18).

## Supported Countries/Regions

This kit for phones and tablets is supported only in the Chinese mainland.

## Emulator Support

Running or debugging using the Emulator is not supported.

# Delivering Digital Products

#### Use Cases

-   Consumables
    
    If your app offers consumables, make sure it delivers the consumables according to the requirements in [Consumables or Non-consumables](#section11425856171915).
    
-   Non-consumables
    
    If your app is a standalone app and offers non-consumables, it should deliver products according to [standalone app product delivery rules](#section82941432162019) (for non-consumables). In other scenarios, it is recommended that products be delivered according to [product delivery rules](#section11425856171915) for consumables or non-consumables.
    
-   Auto-renewable subscriptions
    
    If your app is not a standalone app, you are advised to deliver products according to [product delivery rules](#section3412191542012) for auto-renewable subscriptions. If it is a standalone app, you are advised to follow [standalone app product delivery rules](#section82941432162019) for auto-renewable subscriptions.
    
-   Non-renewing subscriptions
    
    If your app offers non-renewing subscriptions, make sure it delivers them according to the requirements in [Non-renewing Subscriptions](#section1698504812311).

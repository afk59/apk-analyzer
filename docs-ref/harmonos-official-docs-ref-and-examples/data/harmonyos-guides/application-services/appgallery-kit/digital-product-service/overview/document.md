# Overview

The digital product service provides a seamless and efficient integration and interaction process, allowing you to focus on your app's capabilities and achieve business monetization. The service provides basic transaction capabilities for digital products to ensure the completion of user transactions. It also reviews the availability of these capabilities, and product value and quality within your app. In addition, it helps you implement digital product distribution and marketing (including product display and sales in your app and product distribution on AppGallery), manage digital product pricing and discounts, and query services associated with a product, as well as assists in after-sales services in a centralized way.

You can integrate and use AppGallery server capabilities and system-level APIs of IAP to quickly boot the digital product transaction process. This allows you to implement the functions and scenarios for selling digital products in your app. Through digital product transactions, users can buy a wide range of **digital products**, including consumables, non-consumables, auto-renewable subscriptions, and non-reviewing subscriptions.

# About This Kit

AppGallery Kit opens capabilities of AppGallery, enhancing app downloads, recommendations, and distribution to boost exposure on AppGallery. It also offers a seamless and efficient process for integrating and interacting with the digital product service, supporting commercial monetization.

AppGallery Kit provides the following functions and services for your app and in-app digital products:

**[Digital product service](../digital-product-service/document.md)**: Huawei provides platform-based capabilities and services for you to manage, trade, and offer after-sales support for digital products, facilitating transactions and settlements.

**[Recommendation on AppGallery](../recommendation-on-appgallery/document.md)**: Users can directly access your app's details page or the page for adding your atomic service widget to the home screen, effectively increasing exposure of your app or atomic service.

**[On-demand feature distribution](../on-demand-feature-distribution/document.md)**: This function enables independent evolution of multiple sub-services within an app, offering dynamic distribution and resource splitting to improve distribution efficiency.

**[Ecosystem query service](../ecosystem-query-service/document.md)**: You can query the scenes in which your atomic service or app is opened, allowing you to design and expand business operations based on these insights.

**[Update on AppGallery](../update-on-appgallery/document.md)**: This service allows you to check whether there is a new version for your app. If a new version is available, you can use this service to display in-app update reminders for users.

**[App attribution service](../app-attribution-service/document.md)**: This service helps you identify the reasons why users download and use your app, analyze the marketing effect based on the attribution data, evaluate the business value of multiple channels, and optimize business strategies accordingly.

**[Privacy agreement management service](../privacy-agreement-management-service/document.md)**: This service provides users with a privacy agreement pop-up and allows you to query privacy agreement URLs, check the privacy agreement signing status, and revoke records of privacy agreements that users have previously consented to.

**[Icon management service](../icon-management-service/document.md)**: You can use this service to manage custom icons, including querying available custom icons, switching to a custom icon, and reverting to the default icon.

**[App review service](../app-review-service/document.md)**: You can use this service to launch a review pop-up within your app for users to rate and comment on it.

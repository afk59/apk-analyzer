# What Are the Rules for Renewal Periods of a Subscription?

Currently, the following renewal periods are supported: 1 week, 1 month, 2 months, 3 months, 6 months, 1 year, 30 days (last subscription date + 30 days), and 31 days (last subscription date + 31 days). For details, please refer to [Configuring Auto-renewable Subscriptions](../../digital-product-service/configuring-digital-products/about-digital-products/document.md#section167197586468).

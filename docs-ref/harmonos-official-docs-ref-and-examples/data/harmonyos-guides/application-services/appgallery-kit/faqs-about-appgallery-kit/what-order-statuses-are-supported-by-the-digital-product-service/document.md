# What Order Statuses Are Supported by the Digital Product Service?

1.  For details about statuses of a subscription order, please refer to [SubscriptionStatus](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/en/doc/harmonyos-references/iap-query-subscription-status#section9100101211811).
2.  For details about statuses of a consumable or non-consumable order, please refer to [finishStatus](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/en/doc/harmonyos-references/iap-server-data-model#section1950214943118) and [purchaseOrderRevocationReasonCode](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/en/doc/harmonyos-references/iap-server-data-model#section1950214943118).

# Incorrect Classification or Categorization

#### Incorrect App Categorization

If your app has not been released, sign in to [AppGallery Connect](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/en/service/josp/agc/index.html), click **Apps**, find and click your app, and change its category. For details about app categories, please refer to [Categorization](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/en/doc/50103).

#### Incorrect Notification Message Classification

You can perform operations in [Message Self-Classification](../../preparations/applying-for-scenario-specific-message-permissions/document.md#section16708911111611) as long as your push messages comply with [Push Notification Violation Penalty Criteria](../../appendixes/push-notification-violation-penalty-criteria/document.md).

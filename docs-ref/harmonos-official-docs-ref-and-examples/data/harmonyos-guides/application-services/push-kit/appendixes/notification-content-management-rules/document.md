# Notification Content Management Rules

#### Purpose

The rules are formulated to maintain legal, accurate, consistent, and readable notification content sent through Push Kit, and to enhance user experience.

#### Definition

Push notifications are app messages that appear in the notification panel, on the lock screen, and as banner notifications.

Push content encompasses all visual and interactive elements displayed within a push notification. This includes, but is not limited to, the message box, text (title and body), images, app icon, redirection action, and redirection target landing page.

The following figure shows the elements.

-   Message box: including the box border, background, and layout.
-   Push text: all text content within a notification, including the title and body.
-   Redirection action: action after a push message is tapped, for example, launch an app.
-   Landing page: destination page to be redirected to when a push message is tapped.

#### Notification Content Principles

Push notification content should adhere to principles of consistency, accuracy, readability, and compliance to provide users with valuable notification content, thereby increasing user engagement and improving user satisfaction.

## Consistency

The content of push notifications must align with the app's core business, and elements in the content must be consistent with the app's basic information registered with AppGallery, including the icon, brand name, and product name. This rule does not apply to news apps, as their content often includes icons and names of other apps.

1.  The content that a push message leads to should align with what the push message has described, without inducing or forcing users to tap on the push message. Possible inconsistencies include but are not limited to:
    -   Title-content mismatch: For example, the title is unclear or cannot accurately reflect the landing page content, or elements within the notification are not logically connected, which can be misleading.
    -   Deceptive notification: The content of the landing page that the user is directed to after tapping the push notification is unrelated to the content of the notification, for instance, misrepresenting an app push message as an app system notification, communication message, or key notification, such as a friend's interaction, a missed call, or a new message, while in reality the landing page simply opens the app or directs users to irrelevant content.
    -   Misleading benefits: The push notification promotes benefits that do not match those provided on the landing page. All forms of benefits and discounts offered in push notifications must be directly credited to the user's account upon tapping the notification, and must be marked with a clear indication of benefit. It is prohibited to push benefits that require additional user actions without clearly indicating the acquisition method, or to use misleading or deceptive means to promote such benefits.
2.  The landing page content should not be changed.

## Accuracy

An app should push only authentic and objective content. False, deceptive, or exaggerated content is prohibited.

1.  Push notifications must not mislead users by impersonating Huawei or other apps, imitating message boxes with special functions, or using misleading background images (such as imitating red envelopes, envelopes, or missed calls).
2.  An app should not push false or untrue content, such as:
    -   Content that contradicts scientific principles or common knowledge.
    -   Content that does not align with the facts.
    -   Fabricated and false information.
3.  An app should not push clickbait content, such as:
    -   Gross exaggeration: Deliberately exaggerating feelings, scope, results, or degrees to create a sensational effect.
    -   Unnecessary suspense: Deliberately building suspense without providing a resolution.
    -   Manipulative content: Content that employs manipulative tactics, such as threats or ultimatums, to compel user engagement.
4.  An app should not push outdated social news, such as:
    -   News content with a significant time lag between the event and the publication.
    -   Outdated information about a news event after new developments have occurred.
    -   Content with a discrepancy between the timestamp in the text and the time of the push notification.
5.  An app should not push content that contains a false redirection, such as:
    -   Button-shaped images or symbols that cannot implement their described functions when tapped.
    -   Intentionally obscuring, blurring, making overly transparent, disguising, or masking functional redirections, or implementing functions that do not comply with the redirection descriptions. For example, notification taps result in forced downloads, landing pages have delayed automatic downloads, and any tap on the landing page leads to unintended downloads.

## Readability

1.  An app should not push test messages or invalid messages to users.
2.  The pushed text should be presented as complete sentences or phrases with clear meaning, free from grammatical errors, to avoid user misunderstanding, for example, dividing a complete sentence between the title and the main body, or sending batch messages with incomplete or ambiguous content.
3.  The push content should use words and phrases correctly and avoid using idioms, Chinese acronyms, non-standard English abbreviations, or non-common Chinese words, phrases, and idioms.
4.  The push content should use Chinese characters correctly and avoid using pinyin alone, typos, obsolete variant characters, or simplified characters that have been abolished by China. Quotation marks should be used to indicate a character when used homophonically.
5.  The push content should avoid mixing or misusing full-width and half-width punctuation marks.
6.  Images should be clear, legible, and free from defects such as mosaic effects, distortions, or blurriness that may compromise user experience.
7.  A push notification should not be used as a billboard. If the first screen of a landing page that the push notification links to contains ad content, the ad content should not occupy more than half of the user's device screen.

## Compliance

Do not use the following content in a push notification:

1.  Content that compromises national security, divulges state secrets, subverts state power, harms national unity, or impairs the state honor and interests.
2.  Content that involves the use or disguised use of the national flag, national anthem, national emblem, military flag, military song, military emblem, or the name or image of a state organ or its staff member of the People's Republic of China.
3.  Content that involves the use of images of the Communist Party of China and national leaders (including cartoon images) in marketing materials, including those of current, former, and deceased party or national leaders.
4.  Content that distorts, demonizes, blasphemes, or denies the deeds and spirits of heroic martyrs, or insults, slanders, or otherwise infringes upon the names, portraits, reputations, or honors of heroic martyrs.
5.  Content that undermines social stability, harms the public interest, spreads rumors, disrupts economic and social order, promotes terrorism or extremism, or incites terrorist or extremist activities.
6.  Content that endangers personal safety or property, discloses personal privacy, insults or defames others, or infringes upon others' reputation, privacy, or other lawful rights.
7.  Content that disturbs the public order or good customs and public morals, or departs from a good social climate.
8.  Content that uses exaggerated headlines, headlines that seriously mismatch the content, or vulgar and provocative clickbait headlines.
9.  Content that spreads or describes obscenity, pornography, gambling, violence, murder, or terror, or abets crimes.
    -   Content that directly shows or depicts human sex organs or covers only a small part of human bodies.
    -   Content that depicts sexual acts, and sexually suggestive or insulting content in an explicit or implicit manner.
    -   Content that describes sexual acts, processes, or methods using sexually suggestive or provocative language.
    -   Content that includes any material that invades an individual's privacy, such as up-skirting, secretly taking photos, or revealing private parts.
    -   Content that contains comics, animations, websites, images, and links relevant to pornography, or pornographic text, audio, and video content prohibited by relevant authorities, including deleted clips of movies and TV series.
    -   Content that contains sexual innuendo, sexual provocation, or other material that may be sexually suggestive, or that promotes vulgar, lowbrow, or sensational content.
    -   Content that publicizes or describes improper dating information such as one-night stand, wife swapping, and sadomasochism.
    -   Content that shows blood, thriller, or cruelty that causes physical or mental discomfort.
10.  Content that contains ethnic, racial, religious, or gender discrimination, incites ethnic hatred or discrimination, undermines national unity, or incites group or regional discrimination.
11.  Content that disrupts the religious policies of the country or publicizes evil cults or feudal and superstitious ideologies.
12.  Content that impedes the protection of environment, natural resources, or cultural heritages.
13.  Content that contains improper comments on disasters such as natural disasters and major accidents. Content related to sudden events or disasters should avoid sensationalizing gory scenes or dwelling on gruesome details.
14.  Content that contains the hype of rumors, scandals, misdeeds, entertainment gossip, celebrity gossip, bloody violence, strange stories, vulgar and indecent content, and other content that contravene the socialist core values.
15.  Content that exaggerates and widely spreads the statements made by immoral artists and individuals involved in negative controversies.
16.  Content that may cause minors to imitate unsafe behavior, violate social morality, or induce minors to have bad hobbies.
17.  Content that discloses personal information or data, including but not limited to: name, age, date of birth, gender, nationality, occupation, employer, education, family member information, home address and corresponding postal code, and location data.
18.  Content that infringes upon the legitimate rights and interests of third parties, such as trade secrets and copyrights.
19.  Other content prohibited by laws and administrative regulations, as well as that has a negative impact on the online ecosystem.

Rules for pushing news information are as follows:

-   Tools apps that have not obtained an _Internet News Information Service License_ should not push news information.
-   Only news information sourced from authorized channels can be pushed. Illegal editing, publishing, or reprinting of news is forbidden.
-   An app should not push news information that sensationalizes or hypes public opinion hotspots, takes quotes out of context, alters original meanings, or misleads netizens.

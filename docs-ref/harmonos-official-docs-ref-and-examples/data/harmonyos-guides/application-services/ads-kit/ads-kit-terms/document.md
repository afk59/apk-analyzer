# Ads Kit Terms

#### Banner Ad

A banner ad is a rectangular image displayed at the top, middle, or bottom of an in-app page. It automatically refreshes at an interval.

#### Interstitial Ad

Interstitial ads appear in either full or half screen when an app is launched, paused, or closed. They are cleverly timed to not disrupt the user's regular interaction with the app. Due to their large size, they offer excellent visibility and exposure.

#### Native Ad

Native ads can be images or videos, which are less disruptive and fit seamlessly into the surrounding content to match your custom app design.

#### OAID

An Open Anonymous Device Identifier (OAID) is a non-permanent device identifier. You can use OAIDs to provide personalized ads for users while protecting their privacy. In addition, third-party tracking platforms can provide conversion attributions for advertisers based on OAIDs.

#### Rewarded Ad

Rewarded ads are full-screen video ads that allow users to view in exchange for in-app rewards.

#### Roll Ad

Roll ads are displayed as short videos or images, before, during, or after the video content is played.

#### Splash Ad

Splash ads are displayed immediately after an app is launched, even before the home screen of the app is displayed. You need to design a default slogan image for the app in advance, and ensure that the default slogan image is displayed before a splash ad is loaded, enhancing user experience.

#### Petal Ads

Petal Ads is a comprehensive intelligent marketing platform within the HarmonyOS ecosystem. It integrates media traffic across Huawei's 1+8+N all-scenario ecosystem, delivering a high-quality marketing experience for users.

#### eCPM

Effective cost per mille (eCPM) indicates the ad revenue obtained from one thousand views or impressions.

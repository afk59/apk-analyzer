Get started

 | 

1.  [Register as a developer](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/en/doc/start/registration-and-verification-0000001053628148), [complete identity verification](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/en/doc/start/rna-0000001062530373), and [create a HarmonyOS app or atomic service](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/en/doc/app/agc-help-createharmonyapp-0000001945392297).
2.  [Apply for Health Service Kit](../development/getting-started/applying-for-health-service-kit/document.md), select the product type, and enable the read and write scopes for the corresponding data.
    
    a. Check your qualifications by referring to [Qualifications Requirements for Developers](../qualifications-requirements-for-developers/document.md). Ensure that you have met all of the requirements specified before applying for scopes.
    
    b. Select scopes based on your data usage scenario, and provide application materials and enterprise introduction as instructed in [Applying for Health Service Kit](../development/getting-started/applying-for-health-service-kit/document.md). The application will be handled within 15 workdays.
    

 |
| 

Develop your app

 | 

Complete the app development as required.

 |
| 

Apply for verification

 | 

Submit an application to get your app verified after the development and testing are complete. Provide video proof and checklists as instructed in [Applying for Formal Scopes](../development/applying-for-formal-scopes/document.md). After receiving your application, we will review it and verify the scopes within 15 workdays. Once approved, your app can be put into large-scale commercial use.

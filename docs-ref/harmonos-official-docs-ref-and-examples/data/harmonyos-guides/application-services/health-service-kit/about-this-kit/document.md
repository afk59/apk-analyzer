# About This Kit

Health Service Kit provides a platform for ecosystem apps to access users' health and fitness data based on users' HUAWEI ID and authorization. Upon obtaining user's consent and authorization, you can access their health and fitness data with the open capabilities provided by Health Service Kit, and use these data to build various kinds of apps and services to deliver professional health and fitness experiences.

#### App Services

Health Service Kit incorporates its health and fitness data services with apps, services, and third-party devices, to construct a smart health and fitness ecosystem that provides users with unmatched convenience and security.

App services include the following:

-   Huawei apps:
    -   The Huawei Health app provides professional features such as workout recording, courses for weight loss and body shaping, TruSleep, and health management.
    -   The AI Life app is a unified management platform for Huawei smart IoT devices. It can discover, connect, and manage Huawei smart 8 + N devices to implement interconnections between smart devices, creating dedicated smart scenarios and making life better.

-   Ecosystem apps: Ecosystem apps in fields such as sports & fitness, medical research, and insurance, build a professional scenario-based user experience based on Huawei smart wearable devices and the Health Service Kit data platform.

#### Health Service Kit

As shown in the preceding figure, Health Service Kit provides a series of open capabilities, for ecosystem apps/services and hardware devices. It provides a wide range of health and fitness APIs and supports openness of various health and fitness data, such as step count and heart rate.

-   Open data: As a health and fitness data platform, this kit unlocks extensive health data and professional workout and fitness data.
-   Open services: This kit supports workout linking and real-time data syncing between wearable devices and ecosystem devices (coming soon).
-   Open models: In addition to the basic health and fitness data, this kit provides models that perform analysis on such indicators as maximum oxygen uptake (VO2Max) and training load, giving users more insights into their overall wellness (coming soon).
-   Data platform: This kit provides a data platform for you to store health and fitness data.
-   Privacy compliance for data security: This kit complies with applicable laws and regulations to manage privacy. Full lifecycle management for personal data is performed by referring to the GAPP and other best practices in the industry.

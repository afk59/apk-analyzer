# Enabling Live View Kit

1.  Sign in to [AppGallery Connect](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/en/service/josp/agc/index.html) and click **Development and services**.
    
    ![](https://contentcenter-vali-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/pvt_2/DeveloperAlliance_scene_100_1/45/v3/fALAQDz4TT2gcynBtQ73qA/en-us_image_0000002543551667.png?HW-CC-KV=V1&amp;HW-CC-Date=20260410T062110Z&amp;HW-CC-Expire=86400&amp;HW-CC-Sign=45089F005D40CB3F2EFA48BD6DC09AF890796CF004D7AD1D8A0D02C4C8E12D51 "Click to enlarge")
    
2.  Find your project and click the app for which you want to enable Live View Kit.
    
    ![](https://contentcenter-vali-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/pvt_2/DeveloperAlliance_scene_100_1/87/v3/W7UenV7-SDGaLEHSsG5tJA/en-us_image_0000002543471663.png?HW-CC-KV=V1&amp;HW-CC-Date=20260410T062110Z&amp;HW-CC-Expire=86400&amp;HW-CC-Sign=C246FB8087B801C4F4737D01FD2888E295845004474912181AC3DE88EBF53D3C "Click to enlarge")
    
3.  Go to **Project settings** > **Manage open capabilities**, and click **Request** next to **Live View Kit**.
    
    ![](https://contentcenter-vali-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/pvt_2/DeveloperAlliance_scene_100_1/85/v3/zhr8G08CTtefAO6SVQ4Tqg/en-us_image_0000002512031720.png?HW-CC-KV=V1&amp;HW-CC-Date=20260410T062110Z&amp;HW-CC-Expire=86400&amp;HW-CC-Sign=ED7D13799CE5C3C398B8D1F71095CC44B1DB6AA6D772D8BF866A38A6A5FDCA05 "Click to enlarge")
    
4.  Provide the required information, including the scenarios, scenario description, and attachment according to the template in **Request reason**, and click **Submit**.
    
    ![](https://contentcenter-vali-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/pvt_2/DeveloperAlliance_scene_100_1/a7/v3/-suPzLp_TM643ITzElSAdA/en-us_image_0000002512031730.png?HW-CC-KV=V1&amp;HW-CC-Date=20260410T062110Z&amp;HW-CC-Expire=86400&amp;HW-CC-Sign=E3D3B64F983C0F7CA3544EEB196C97E5972BAFC1C9B006FEA1F9C69025F6FBAD "Click to enlarge")
    
5.  You will receive the Live View Kit request result within five working days. Please check the service enabling request information in the interaction center for updates.
    
    ![](https://contentcenter-vali-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/pvt_2/DeveloperAlliance_scene_100_1/a6/v3/nC8f4CdESRaTEbecOhx7iw/en-us_image_0000002543551683.png?HW-CC-KV=V1&amp;HW-CC-Date=20260410T062110Z&amp;HW-CC-Expire=86400&amp;HW-CC-Sign=D26B9083B48BBBB32502CCCA7417D16FD758BBD55CE3928E8CBBE93241444BAD "Click to enlarge")
    
    ![](https://contentcenter-vali-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/pvt_2/DeveloperAlliance_scene_100_1/17/v3/FrJ_rzFDRjulF7TT5c4Klw/en-us_image_0000002543551681.png?HW-CC-KV=V1&amp;HW-CC-Date=20260410T062110Z&amp;HW-CC-Expire=86400&amp;HW-CC-Sign=32AAE837810B748B7A955FB308DDAB723D7A9888767DB008F03F6206A121B7A7 "Click to enlarge")
    
6.  After your Live View Kit request is approved, go to the **Certificates, app IDs, and profiles** page, click **Profiles** on the left, click **Add** to generate a new profile, and download the profile to the local host. When [releasing your app](../../../../app-release/release/document.md), you need to package the profile into the app package.
    
    ![](https://contentcenter-vali-drcn.dbankcdn.cn/pvt_2/DeveloperAlliance_scene_100_1/61/v3/m0ZLJMBsT2W7DaqM-4D6iQ/note_3.0-en-us.png?HW-CC-KV=V1&amp;HW-CC-Date=20260410T062110Z&amp;HW-CC-Expire=86400&amp;HW-CC-Sign=2DA1C6D92B45ACE8CE08D1C0689791903270444C48645195E668F0D4C16008F0)
    
    For details about how to generate a profile, please refer to [Managing Profiles](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/en/doc/app/agc-help-profile-0000002270709473). For details about how to package a profile into an app package, please refer to [Configuring Signature Information](../../../../app-release/release/document.md#section280162182818).

# Benefit Analysis and Reports

#### Self-service Settlement

After your app has sold some products, you can sign to the [HUAWEI Developers](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/en/) website, click **Console** in the upper right corner, go to **My accounts** > **Credited**. Then, on the **Settlements** tab, you can view your settlement sheets and submit them after confirmation. For more information, please refer to [Self-service Settlement Guide](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/en/doc/start/checkoutguide-0000001053128363).

#### Viewing and Downloading IAP Reports

After your app has sold some products, you can sign to the [HUAWEI Developers](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/en/) website and go to **Developer center** > **My reports** > **HUAWEI IAP** to view or export payment or preferential offer details of the specified time segment.

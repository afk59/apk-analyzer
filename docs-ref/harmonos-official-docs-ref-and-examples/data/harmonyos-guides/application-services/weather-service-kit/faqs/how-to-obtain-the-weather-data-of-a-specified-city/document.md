# How to Obtain the Weather Data of a Specified City

Call the [getAddressesFromLocationName](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/en/doc/harmonyos-references/js-apis-geolocationmanager#geolocationmanagergetaddressesfromlocationname-1) method to obtain the latitude and longitude information of the specified city, and then call the [getWeather](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/en/doc/harmonyos-references/weather-service-weatherservice#section78181857767) method to obtain the weather data based on the returned longitude and latitude data.

# About This Kit

Weather Service Kit is a data provision service in the HarmonyOS ecosystem. With the support of multiple top meteorological suppliers, it provides professional, accurate, and stable ultra-local weather forecast service, helping you provide users with more considerate local life services and also enable industry customers to prevent and mitigate disasters, reduce costs, and improve efficiency. You can easily obtain weather data by integrating this kit with your HarmonyOS apps or atomic services.

#### Use Case

Weather Service Kit can return the following weather data to meet your requirements:

-   City-level and POI-level weather forecasts: real-time weather, 48-hour weather forecasts by hour (cities outside China: 24-hour weather forecasts), and 10-day weather forecasts. Comprehensive weather data, such as weather conditions, temperature, wind strength, wind direction, and humidity, is provided.
-   Minute-level precipitation forecasts for the next 2 hours within 5 km: including precipitation patterns and intensity.
-   Weather alerts: real-time weather and environment alerts released by the local meteorological bureau.
-   Weather-related indices: including drying index, dressing index, UV index, road condition index, and fishing index in the next three days.
-   Astronomical data: sunrise time, sunset time, moonrise time, moonset time, moon phase, etc.
-   Tide: tide data on major local ports and cities.

#### Restrictions

-   Usage restrictions: Weather Service Kit is currently available only to system apps and cannot be accessed by external apps.
-   Device restrictions: This kit is only applicable to phones, tablets, PCs/2-in-1 devices, and wearables.

#### Emulator Support

This kit is not supported on the Emulator.

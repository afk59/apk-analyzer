# Preparations on Your Server

1.  Connect your app to payment service providers (such as Huawei Pay, WeChat Pay, Alipay, and UnionPay) to complete order payment for card activation and top-up. For details about the connection solution, please refer to the access description of the specific payment service provider.
2.  Connect your app to the SP TSM systems of transportation card companies to deliver card activation, top-up, update, and deletion tasks. For details about the connection APIs, please refer to the SP TSM system access description of the specific card company.

# About This Kit

Wallet Kit integrates the open capabilities of the end-to-end "chip-device-cloud" technology, providing digital passes such as car keys and transportation cards. Users can easily store these digital passes on their mobile phones for convenient use whenever needed.

#### Use Cases

-   Car key

A user can enable a digital car key in HUAWEI Wallet to use their phone to unlock their car through Bluetooth, instead of using a physical key. A digital car key can be easily shared with others (such as family members). It will not be long before this technology is widely applied in the automotive industry of the Chinese mainland.

-   Transportation card

A user can activate a transportation card on their phone to replace the physical transportation card. They can then tap their phone to ride on subways or buses.

#### Supported Countries and Regions

This kit is temporarily available only in the Chinese mainland.

#### Restrictions on Devices

This kit is only applicable to phones.

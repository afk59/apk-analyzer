# Why does the boot animation still have sound during a restart when the phone is set to silent mode?

Specifications: The phone has multiple types of volume settings. Typically, setting the phone to silent mode means that the media volume is muted. The sound of the boot animation is controlled by the ringtone volume. If you want to disable the sound of the boot animation, you can set the ringtone volume to **0**, or turn off the boot ringtone in the Settings menu.

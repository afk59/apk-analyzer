# How do I switch audio application development from OpenSL ES to OHAudio?

You are advised to transition from OpenSL ES to OHAudio for audio development due to the limitations of OpenSL ES in meeting the advanced capabilities required by the audio system. For details, see [Switching from OpenSL ES to OHAudio](https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/en/doc/harmonyos-guides/replace-opensles-by-ohaudio).

# How do I export screenshots from a device to a local PC?

Use the screenshot feature of DevEco Studio.

1.  Connect the device to the PC.
2.  Click the **Log** tab at the bottom of DevEco Studio. From the toolbar on the left, click the camera icon to take a screenshot and save it to a specified path.

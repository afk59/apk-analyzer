# What should I do if DevEco Studio fails to connect to the device after the ROM of the device is upgraded to 5.0.0.31 or later?

**Symptom**

After the ROM of a device is upgraded to 5.0.0.31 or later, DevEco Studio failed to connect to the device and cannot detect it.

**Solution**

If the username on the PC where DevEco Studio is used is in Chinese, change it to English or Pinyin. Then, try reconnecting the device.

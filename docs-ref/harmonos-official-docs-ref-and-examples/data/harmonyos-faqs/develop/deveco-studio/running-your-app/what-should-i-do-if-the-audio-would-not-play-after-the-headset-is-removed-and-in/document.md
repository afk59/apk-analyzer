# What should I do if the audio would not play after the headset is removed and inserted during playback?

**Symptom**

After removing the headset and then inserting it into the USB port, the audio playback feature of the Emulator becomes invalid.

**Solution**

The Emulator does not support headset hot swap. Do not remove or insert the headset during audio playback. If the audio cannot be played after the headset is removed and inserted, restart the Emulator. You are also advised to use a headset with a circular jack to avoid this problem.

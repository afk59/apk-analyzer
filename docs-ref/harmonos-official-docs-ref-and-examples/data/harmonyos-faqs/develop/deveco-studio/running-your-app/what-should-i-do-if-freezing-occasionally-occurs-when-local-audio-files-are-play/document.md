# What should I do if freezing occasionally occurs when local audio files are played on the Emulator?

**Symptom**

In the following scenarios, the Emulator may encounter audio playback freezing:

Scenario 1: The audio is played immediately after the Emulator is started.

Scenario 2: The audio is played for a long time on the Emulator.

**Solution**

Perform the audio test after the Emulator keeps running stably. For a long-time verification of audio playback, you are advised to use a real device.
